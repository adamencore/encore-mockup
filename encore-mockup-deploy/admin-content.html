FAQs appear under the Audition 101 video on the parent dashboard.

What should kids wear to their audition?
Comfortable clothes they can move in — leggings or shorts, a fitted t-shirt, athletic shoes (no sandals or boots). Hair pulled back so directors can see their face.
How long will the audition take?
Plan for 90 minutes. The individual slot is 5 minutes, but the dance call runs in groups so they'll need to stay until everyone in their age group has gone.
What if my child is nervous?
Totally normal! Three things help: practice the cut at home until they feel solid, arrive 15 minutes early to settle in, and remember that we want them to do well.
When will we hear about casting?
Cast lists are usually posted 5–7 days after auditions close. You'll get a notification here and an email. Callbacks (if needed) happen within 48 hours of the audition.
Can my child audition for multiple shows?
Yes! Many kids audition for two or more shows per season. Just be honest about conflicts so directors can plan around them.
